## Connectors

**Overview of the WATCHPAX 64 connector panel.**

![WATCHPAX 64 Connectors - Front](../media/wp64/introduction_03.jpg)
![WATCHPAX 64 Connectors - Rear](../media/wp64/introduction_04.jpg)

:::warning
Do not connect a network from your switch, router or network jack to either of the RJ45 ports on the sync card (no. 14 & 15 in the picture above), as these ports are reserved for sync data only. The sync card will not operate if a network is connected to any of those ports.
:::
