## Dimensions

**Physical dimensions and weight of the WATCHPAX 64 unit.**

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Width | 446 mm (17.6 in) |
| Height | 88 mm (3.5 in) — 2U rack height |
| Depth | 336 mm (13.2 in) |
| Weight | Approx. 10 kg (22 lbs) |

The WATCHPAX 64 occupies **2U** of vertical space in a standard 19-inch rack. An optional rack kit is available for rack mounting — see [Installation](../02-installation-and-operation/03-installation.md#installation-in-19-inch-rack) for details.
