## Accessories

**A summary of items included with the WATCHPAX 64 and accessories available separately.**

### Included

* 1 x IEC 60320 10A/250V AC power cord with C13 connector
* 4 x DisplayPort 1.4 to HDMI 2.0b adapters
* Rubber feet
* With SDI capture card option: 9x Micro BNC to BNC adapters

### Available Separately

* Kit for mounting in 19-inch rack
* Micro BNC to BNC adapters
* DisplayPort 1.4 to HDMI 2.0b adapters
