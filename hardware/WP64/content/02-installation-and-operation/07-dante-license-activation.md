## Dante License Activation

**WATCHPAX 64 is Dante-ready for WATCHOUT 7.** This means the Dante license must be purchased and activated by the user.

:::warning
The Dante license is bonded to the selected network adapter. After activation, the license cannot be transferred to another port.
:::

### Activate Dante on WATCHPAX 64

1. In WATCHOUT 7 Producer, create an Audio Device and enable it in the audio device properties. Select the network adapter that will be used for Dante.
2. Open Dante Controller. The device should appear as **Dante Ready**.
3. Click the Dante Activator icon and sign in.
4. Purchase and activate the Dante license in Dante Activator. Internet access is required during purchase and activation.
5. Return to WATCHOUT 7 Producer, open the node properties for the device, and restart the device to back up the Dante license.
6. Confirm that Dante is activated, then map audio channels in Dante Controller.

:::info
After the restart backup, the Dante license can be recovered after factory reset, including offline recovery scenarios.
:::

For additional details, see the Dataton knowledge base article: [How to activate Dante license on my WATCHOUT 7 media server](https://knowledge.dataton.com/knowledge/how-to-activate-dante-license-on-my-watchpax-media-server).
