## Installation

**Instructions for installing and powering your WATCHPAX 30.**

### General

* This equipment is for professional use for installation at locations where only adults are normally present. Check the "Safety First" list before use.
* The WATCHPAX 30 must be connected to a properly grounded wall socket (a socket-outlet with protective earth connection in the building).
* The serial number is located on the base of the unit.

:::warning
Only use the power cord supplied with the WATCHPAX 30 unit, otherwise Dataton AB cannot guarantee full functionality.
:::

### Standalone Installation

Place the unit flat on its base. The base is the side of the unit showing the serial number and Dataton logo.

### Power On

The unit is switched on by inserting the power cord or inserting the power cord and using the on/off switch. The discreet on/off switch is located behind the front plate of the WATCHPAX 30 (see [Connectors](../01-introduction/08-connectors.md) in the Introduction).

:::warning
Use a pencil or similar insulated tool to press the on/off switch. Do not use a metal implement.
:::

### First Power On

The first time you power up a WATCHPAX 30 (after delivery or after a reset) the system will finalize installation and reboot several times. This procedure will typically take about 5 minutes to complete.

:::warning
Do not interrupt this procedure.
:::

### Power Off

Powering down should be initiated from within WATCHOUT Producer software or by using the on/off switch.

When the power-off sequence is complete, the fans will turn off, and the power cord may be removed.

:::tabs
### From WATCHOUT 7 Producer

1. In the *Nodes* window, right-click on the WATCHPAX 30 display device.
2. Select **Shutdown**.
3. Wait for the unit to fully power down before removing the power cord.

For more information, refer to chapter 9.1: Node List in the "WATCHOUT 7 User's Guide".

### From WATCHOUT 6 Producer

1. In WATCHOUT 6, choose one of the displays from the WATCHPAX 30 media server.
2. On the Stage menu, choose **Manage Display Computer** then **Remote Access**.
3. On the File menu, choose **Shut Down**.
4. Wait for the unit to fully power down before removing the power cord.

### Using the On/Off Switch

1. Press the on/off switch located behind the front plate of the WATCHPAX 30 (see [Connectors](../01-introduction/08-connectors.md)).
2. Use a pencil or similar insulated tool to press the switch — do not use a metal implement.
3. Wait for the unit to fully power down before removing the power cord.
:::

:::info
If the unit is unresponsive and cannot be shut down via software or the on/off switch, unplug the power cord as a last resort to force a shutdown. Be aware that this may cause data loss or system corruption.
:::

:::warning
Do not unplug the power cord during a normal power-off sequence, as this may cause data loss and system corruption.
:::
