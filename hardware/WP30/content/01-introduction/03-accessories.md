## Accessories

**Items included with your WATCHPAX 30.**

* **Power cord** — IEC C6 to regional mains connector for powering the unit. Only use the supplied power cord to ensure full functionality and warranty coverage.
* **Four EDID emulators** — Small inline adapters that plug into the HDMI outputs to maintain stable display identification data, ensuring consistent resolution and timing even when a display is temporarily disconnected. See [EDID Emulators](../03-synchronization/01-edid-emulators.md) for installation and configuration instructions.
