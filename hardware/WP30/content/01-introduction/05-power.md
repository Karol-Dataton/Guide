## Power

**Power inlet specifications for the WATCHPAX 30.**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Connector | IEC C6 power inlet |
| Input voltage | 100 – 240V AC |
| Max power consumption | 60 W |
| Typical power consumption | 30–40 W |

A power cord is included with the WATCHPAX 30. See [Accessories](03-accessories.md) for a full list of included items.

:::warning
Only use the power cord supplied with the WATCHPAX 30 unit, otherwise Dataton AB cannot guarantee full functionality.
:::
