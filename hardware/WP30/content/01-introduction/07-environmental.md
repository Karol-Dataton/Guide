## Environmental

**Operating and storage environmental specifications for the WATCHPAX 30.**

### Temperature Range

| Condition | Range |
|---|---|
| Optimal ambient temperature for operation | 21°C to 23°C |
| Operating | 0°C to +35°C |
| Storage and transportation | -20°C to +40°C |

### Relative Humidity

| Condition | Range |
|---|---|
| Optimal | 45% to 50% |
| Operating | 20% to 85% (non-condensing) |
| Storage and transportation | 10% to 90% (non-condensing) |

### Altitude

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| Operating, maximum | 2000 meters above sea level |
