# PLAYBACK

**Playback is where your show leaves the editing environment and reaches the audience - whether that means previewing on your Producer screen or running live across a network of display servers.** This chapter covers the full path from local preview through network connection to live show operation, including the run/pause/stop state model, online mode, and procedures for reliable performance.

### Getting Started

Understand how WATCHOUT controls playback before going live.

- [Starting and Stopping](01-starting-and-stopping.md) - the per-timeline run, pause, and stop states, keyboard shortcuts, and how playback commands propagate to connected display servers
- [Preview Mode](02-preview-mode.md) - validating timing, transitions, and composition flow directly in Producer before committing to full system output

### Going Live

Connect Producer to your display network and bring the system online.

- [Connecting to Display Servers](03-connecting-to-display-servers.md) - discovering Runner nodes on the network, assigning them to displays, and verifying connectivity
- [Going Online](04-going-online.md) - transitioning from offline editing to live operation with Director, Runner, and Asset Manager services active

### Show Operation

Running your show reliably in front of an audience.

- [Running Your Show](05-running-your-show.md) - repeatable procedures for rehearsal, performance, multi-timeline coordination, and operator best practices
