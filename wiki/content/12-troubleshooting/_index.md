# TROUBLESHOOTING

**When something goes wrong during setup, rehearsal, or performance, systematic diagnosis gets you back on track faster than guesswork.** This chapter organizes solutions by problem category - from common setup issues through display, network, and media playback problems - and provides tools for gathering diagnostic data and escalating to Dataton support when needed.

### Start Here [Common Issues]

Begin troubleshooting with the most frequently encountered issues.

- [Common Issues](01-common-issues.md) - high-frequency problems seen during setup and rehearsal, with quick diagnosis steps and fixes

### By Category

Focused troubleshooting guides organized by problem area.

- [Performance Tips](02-performance-tips.md) - diagnosing and resolving performance bottlenecks related to media complexity, output load, and node configuration
- [Display Problems](03-display-problems.md) - systematic diagnosis of display issues: routing, output mode, geometry, and content problems
- [Network Issues](04-network-issues.md) - resolving discovery failures, firewall blocks, and host-alias consistency problems
- [Media Playback Issues](05-media-playback-issues.md) - fixing playback errors caused by format compatibility, missing data, or timing and resource pressure

### Getting Support

When you need additional help beyond self-service troubleshooting.

- [Feedback Reports](06-feedback-reports.md) - creating compressed diagnostic archives that bundle logs, system information, and show files for support analysis
- [Getting Help](07-getting-help.md) - gathering diagnostics and contacting Dataton support for efficient issue resolution
