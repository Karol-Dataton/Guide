Combined Brightness Contrast Exposure Gamma
Brightness applies a linear (additive) shift — every pixel moves by the same amount. Exposure is multiplicative — it scales pixels proportionally, so highlights shift more than shadows. Contrast stretches or compresses values around the midpoint. Gamma reshapes the midtone curve using a power function, leaving deep shadows and bright highlights less affected. The transfer curve shows how each transform remaps input to output values.
